# uog-student-referral-system
Web-based student health referral and visit tracking system for the University of Gondar.

It is an electronic health care system that will address the issue of loss of medical
records when patients are transferred from one health care facility to another, as well as when
they visit labs. The system assigns a permanent identifier (UPIN), to each patient and connects
all of their medical history (diagnosis, medication, laboratory tests, and allergy) into one secure
digital file. The system is accessible through the internet and provides a variety of functions to
various individuals that work in different capacities such as receptionist, who register patients,
physicians, who enter clinical information and refer patients to other services, laboratory
technicians, who enter laboratory test results and administrative personnel, who enter and verify
user account information. Access to the clinical information is restricted to those users who are
authorized to view it and perform their job requirements. The purpose of the system is to ensure
that a patient's medical history is available at all times, regardless of where the patient receives
medical treatment.

This project is made for University of Gondar student clinic, for final year project.
